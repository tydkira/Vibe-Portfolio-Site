I went in cold and expected a tidy romance. What I got was something gentler
and sadder: two people measuring the lives they didn’t live against the ones
they did.

Greta Lee and Teo Yoo make silence feel loaded. The New York bar scene near
the end is basically perfect — no speechifying, just the weight of a choice
already made.

**Would I recommend it?** Yes, when you want something intimate instead of
plot-heavy. Pair it with a quiet night; it rewards attention.
