I first saw *Parasite* in a packed theater and mostly felt the shock of the
mid-film turn. This time, at home with the rain going, I noticed how carefully
the jokes set the trap.

Nothing here is accidental: the smell gag, the stairs, the way every favor
comes with a cost. It still lands as entertainment first — which is why the
ending hurts.

**Would I recommend it?** Yes, especially if you only remember the memes.
The craft is the point.
