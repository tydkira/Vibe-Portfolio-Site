Saw this again on a proper screen and remembered why people still talk about
it like a thesis on editing. The chase never really stops, but you always know
what everyone wants.

Charlize Theron’s Furiosa carries the moral center; Max is almost a supporting
player in his own franchise. That choice still feels bold.

**Would I recommend it?** Absolutely — and not just to action fans. It’s one
of the cleanest “show, don’t tell” movies of the last decade.
